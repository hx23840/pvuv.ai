# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Site owners reviewing traffic, visitor journeys, performance and invalid traffic in their self-hosted analytics console. They use the same dashboard in bright daytime workspaces and dim evening rooms; appearance must follow their preference without changing the workflow.

## Product Purpose

Help owners understand audience activity and traffic quality, configure tracking and inspect evidence before changing ad protection. This fork adds light, dark and system appearance to the complete self-hosted edition.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, practical, evidence-led. Preserve the existing system typography, compact controls, blue actions and familiar information hierarchy.

## Anti-references

Avoid a marketing redesign of the console, decorative color changes, and threat-oriented styling that makes ordinary analytics look like a security emergency. The upstream build specification calls for restrained framing and plain HTML.

## Design Principles

- Preserve existing workflows and data semantics.
- Make appearance a personal preference, independent of account and analytics data.
- Keep readable text, charts and status indicators in both themes.
- Use shared tokens so every console page stays consistent.
- Retain the framework-free frontend and small runtime footprint.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native keyboard-operable controls with translated accessible labels. Respect operating-system appearance and reduced-motion preferences. Theme changes must not interrupt focus, navigation, or data loading; storage restrictions must not break the interface.
